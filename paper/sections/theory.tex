\section{Mechanistic Analysis}

The intended mechanism is cut-local complexity reduction. If a permutation
places strongly coupled variables on the same side of most TT cuts, TT-Cross may
require lower intermediate ranks, fewer function-evaluation requests, and lower
peak memory. The argument is not that physical adjacency directly improves
return; it is that ordering changes the family of matricizations that TTPI must
approximate under a finite budget.

\begin{proposition}
Let \(\pi\) be an action-mode ordering, and let
\(\mathcal{M}_k^\pi(A_s)\) denote the unfolding matrix of the conditional
action tensor \(A_s\) at cut \(k\). The exact tensor-chain rank at cut \(k\)
satisfies
\[
r_k^\star(\pi;s)=\mathrm{rank}(\mathcal{M}_k^\pi(A_s)).
\]
If \(A_s\) factorizes according to pairwise interactions and the cross-edge
interaction rank of edge \((i,j)\) is \(\rho_{ij}\), then
\[
r_k^\star(\pi;s)\le \prod_{(i,j)\in\delta_\pi(k)} \rho_{ij}.
\]
\end{proposition}

The first statement is the standard TT rank identity: exact TT ranks are the
matrix ranks of the order-induced unfoldings. The second statement is a
boundary-size upper bound. After contracting all factors strictly to the left
and right of cut \(k\), only interactions crossing the cut need to remain as an
interface, so their transfer dimensions upper-bound the matricization rank.

\begin{lemma}
For weighted cut costs \(C_k^W(\pi)\), the weighted linear-arrangement objective
satisfies
\[
J_{\mathrm{LA}}(\pi)=\sum_{k=1}^{m-1} C_k^W(\pi).
\]
\end{lemma}

This identity makes the role of physics-aware LA precise. LA is not arbitrary:
it measures cumulative crossing mass across all tensor cuts. It is also not a
direct peak-rank objective. Two orderings may have similar or reversed peak
burden even when one has lower total cut area. This is why we evaluate LA,
peak-cut, and rank-aware objectives separately.

\begin{lemma}
Let \(A^V(s,a)\) be the true one-step advantage under value function \(V\), and
let \(\widehat A_\pi(s,a)\) be the ordering-dependent TT approximation. If
\[
\sup_{s,a}|A^V(s,a)-\widehat A_\pi(s,a)|\le \varepsilon_\pi
\]
and \(\widehat \pi_\pi(s)\in\arg\max_a \widehat A_\pi(s,a)\), then
\[
A^V(s,\widehat \pi_\pi(s))\ge \max_a A^V(s,a)-2\varepsilon_\pi .
\]
\end{lemma}

Thus a stronger ordering can improve control only through approximation quality
or finite-budget action search. The diagnostics in this repository are designed
to test the mechanism in three layers: singular-spectrum effective rank as a
mechanistic proxy, actual TT rank profiles as the training artifact, and
memory/runtime/query counts as the observed resource outcome.
