\section{Method}

We define physics-aware mode reordering (PAM) as a family of permutations over
HardMove action modes. The baseline physical layout is
\([acc_0, sw_0, acc_1, sw_1,\ldots]\). Block-preserving PAM treats
\([acc_i, sw_i]\) as an actuator block, builds a block coupling graph, initializes
a spectral order, and applies local search while preserving actuator adjacency.

The rank-aware surrogate used in the repository is
\begin{equation}
J_{\mathrm{rank}}(\pi) =
\lambda_{\mathrm{sum}}\sum_k \log(1 + r_k(\pi)) +
\lambda_{\mathrm{peak}}\max_k \log(1 + r_k(\pi)),
\end{equation}
where \(r_k\) is estimated either from cached singular spectra or from a
segment-coupling proxy.
